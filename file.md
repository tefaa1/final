Abstract

The Learnova project is a pioneering initiative to revolutionize school management by
offering a robust, role-based educational platform that streamlines operations and fosters
collaboration among five key roles: Admin, Manager, Teacher, Parent, and Student.
Educational institutions often face challenges such as fragmented communication,
cumbersome administrative tasks, and limited access to centralized resources, which hinder
academic and operational efficiency. Learnova addresses these challenges with an intuitive
platform that provides tailored functionalities for each role, ensuring a cohesive and engaging
environment. Key features include:

 Role-Based Dashboards: Personalized interfaces for managing tasks specific to each
role, enhancing user experience and productivity.
 Course and Schedule Management: Tools for creating, tracking, and exporting
schedules, including weekly classes, and exams, and virtual classroom integration.
 AI-Powered Chatbot: An NLP-driven assistant supporting English and Arabic,
aiding users with navigation and queries.
 Notification System: Real-time alerts, reminders, and messages with customizable
recipient options, improving communication.
 Performance Analytics: AI and ML-driven insights into student performance,
offering predictive scores and improvement plans.
 Resource Marketplace: A platform for sharing and accessing educational materials,
fostering community engagement.
 Motivation System: A gamified scoring system with badges (Green, Golden,
Diamond) to encourage user participation.
 Multi language and Accessible Design: Support for Arabic and English, with
light/dark modes and a responsive website.
 Attendance Tracking: Real-time monitoring and reporting of student attendance
across classes and semesters.
 Grade Management: Comprehensive tools for uploading, editing, and viewing
grades, supporting both online and offline assessments.
 Activity and Contest Management: Features for creating school-wide events and
contests, with participant tracking and prize allocation.
By integrating these tools into a single, accessible system, Learnova empowers educational
communities to overcome operational challenges, enhance communication, and focus on
academic excellence. We aspire for this project to set a new benchmark for school
management solutions, providing sustainable and impactful support for future generations.

1.3 - Solutions

Traditional Solutions

Traditional approaches to school management often rely on manual coordination and paper-
based systems, placing significant pressure on administrators, teachers, and parents. These
methods, such as handwritten schedules or face-to-face meetings, can lead to
miscommunication and inefficiencies. Additionally, the lack of centralized resource sharing
results in duplicated efforts and limited access to educational materials, while manual
reporting increases workload and delays feedback. Traditional practices may also fail to
engage students or track performance effectively, hindering overall educational outcomes.

Technological Solution

To address these challenges and enhance school management, we developed the Learnova -
Smart School System, a comprehensive technological solution. This platform integrates all
educational stakeholders—Admin, Manager, Teacher, Parent, and Student—into a unified
system to ensure smooth operations and effective communication. It automates administrative
tasks like scheduling, attendance tracking, and grade management, reducing workload and
improving efficiency. The system provides centralized access to resources, including
educational materials and performance analytics, powered by AI to monitor and enhance
student progress. Gamified features and recognition systems boost engagement, while
automated event management and virtual classroom integration optimize activity organization
and remote learning. A resource marketplace facilitates collaboration, and real-time
notifications keep all stakeholders informed. Additionally, parents gain tools to oversee their
child’s academic journey, ensuring comprehensive support for educational success.

Introduction

The Learnova - Smart School System was developed to address the shortcomings of
existing educational platforms and provide a comprehensive solution for modern school
management. By analyzing systems like Classera and Google Classroom, we identified
significant limitations in user engagement, communication, and functionality. This chapter
briefly reviews these platforms, highlights their drawbacks, and compares them with
Learnova, demonstrating how our system offers superior features, including support for five
distinct user roles and enhanced student-teacher interaction, to meet the needs of educational
institutions.

2.1 - Classera

Classera is an e-learning platform used in regions like the Middle East, offering tools for
virtual classrooms and resource sharing. Key features include:

 Virtual classrooms for assignments and discussions.
 Basic gamification through points and rewards.
 Resource marketplace for educational materials.
 Support for multiple languages, including Arabic.
Limitations:

 Limited rules supporting only student and teacher engagement.
 Complex navigation and functionality, requiring extensive training.
 Limited gamification, failing to sustain student engagement.
 Weak performance analytics, lacking predictive or personalized insights.
 Poor customization for diverse educational needs.
 Inadequate communication tools, limiting student-teacher interaction.
 Scalability challenges for smaller schools with limited technical support.
2.2 - Google Classroom

Google Classroom is a free platform integrated with Google Workspace, used for classroom
management. Key features include:

 Assignment creation and distribution.
 Integration with Google Docs and Drive.
 Basic communication via announcements and emails.
 Accessible interface across devices.
Limitations:

 No AI-driven analytics for student performance tracking.
 Absence of gamification or motivation systems.
 Limited customization for user roles or workflows.
 Basic resource sharing without a dedicated marketplace.
 Weak support for multilingual interfaces, especially Arabic.
 Minimal tools for managing school-wide activities or contests.
 Ineffective communication features, hindering student-teacher collaboration.

System Requirement Specification

1 - Functional Requirements :

The system supports five roles (Admin, Manager, Teacher, Student, Parent) with tailored
functionalities:

Onboarding and Authentication

 Onboarding Page: Displays introductory content about Learnova with a "Login"
button redirecting to role selection.
 Role Selection: Allows users to choose their role (Admin, Manager, Teacher, Student,
Parent) and redirects to a role-specific login page.
 Login: Validates credentials using JWT authentication (Express.js backend,
MongoDB). First-time users receive an email verification link.
Admin Role

 Dashboard: Displays system metrics (e.g., user counts, schedules) and an AI-
powered chatbot (Arabic/English).
 Notification System: Sends customizable notifications (Reminder, Alert, Message) to
specific users or groups, stored in MongoDB.
 Members Management: CRUD operations for users (add, edit, delete) with bulk
upload via Excel. Displays role-specific data (e.g., teacher’s assigned classes).
 Term, Course, Grade, and Schedule Management: Manages academic terms,
courses, grades, and schedules with CRUD APIs, ensuring no duplicates. Schedules
are exportable as CSV.
Manager Role

 Dashboard: Shows metrics, grades/absence statistics, and top-ranked users with
badges.
 School Hubs: Manages school-wide events with fields for title, dates, location, and
prizes.
 Absence and Grade Management: Displays attendance reports and allows grade
uploads via Excel/CSV/PDF.
 Virtual Classrooms: Creates and manages virtual rooms with links stored in
MongoDB.
Teacher Role

 Dashboard: Displays assigned courses, notifications, and motivation scores (Green,
Golden, Diamond badges).
 Courses: Manages content (videos, PDFs, assignments, exams) via APIs. Tracks
student interactions.
 Absence and Grades: Records attendance and uploads grades (manual for essays,
automatic for MCQs).
 Activities and Library: Manages contests and uploads materials to a general/subject-
specific library.
Student Role

 Dashboard: Shows scores, attendance, progress bars, and AI-driven performance
predictions.
 Courses: Accesses course content (videos, assignments, exams) and tracks
interactions for motivation scores.
 Absence, Grades, and Schedules: Displays attendance records, grades, and
schedules in responsive tables/charts.
 Activities and Library: Allows joining contests and accessing educational materials.
Parent Role

 Dashboard: Summarizes child’s performance, attendance, grades, and activities.
 Monitoring: Accesses child-specific data (courses, schedules, grades) in a
summarized view.
2 - Non-Functional Requirements :

Technology Stack:
o Frontend: React.js with Tailwind CSS for responsive, accessible UI with
light/dark mode and Arabic/English support.
o Backend: Express.js for RESTful APIs, handling authentication, CRUD
operations, and data processing.
o Database: MongoDB for scalable, schema-flexible storage of users, courses,
schedules, and other data.
o API Testing: Postman and API Dog for developing and testing APIs.
o Authentication: JWT with SECRET_KEY for secure user sessions.
o AI/ML: ML-based chatbot (supporting Arabic/English) and ML model for
student performance analysis, deployed via APIs.
Security:
o JWT authentication ensures secure login and session management.
o HTTPS for all API communications.
o Role-based access control to limit data access per role.
Performance:
o TanStack Query uses an in-memory cache to store the results of server data
fetching, improving performance and reducing unnecessary network requests.
Scalability:
o MongoDB’s horizontal scaling with sharding for large datasets.
o Cloud deployment (e.g., AWS/Google Cloud) with load balancers for traffic
management.
Reliability:
o Error handling for failed API calls with user-friendly toast messages.
o The system shall maintain high availability with minimal downtime, ensuring
consistent access to services for users.
Maintainability:
o Modular React components and Express routes for easy updates.
o Comprehensive API documentation via Postman/API Dog.

Chapter 5

Implementaion and Result

Onboarding Page

The onboarding page provides a guided introduction to the system for first-time users. It
includes a series of short slides or sections explaining the system’s purpose, features, and
benefits

Outcome: This step helps new users understand the platform before logging in, improving
adoption and engagement, especially for students and parents unfamiliar with digital
platforms.

Role Selection Page

After onboarding, users are directed to select their role within the school system. The
platform supports multiple roles such as:

 Student
 Teacher
 Parent
 Manager
 Admin
Each role selection personalizes the user experience and controls access to specific features
based on their permissions.

Outcome: By selecting their role early, users are redirected to the correct login flow and later
see a role-specific dashboard. This helps simplify navigation and improves security by
enforcing role-based access control.

Login Page

The login page allows users to access their accounts using their registered email and
password. The interface is clean and responsive, and it provides real-time validation to
prevent common input errors. Upon successful login, users are redirected to their dashboard
based on their role.

Security Features:

 Input validation (e.g., valid email format)
 Secure password handling
 Feedback for incorrect credentials
 Session handling for authenticated access
Outcome: The login system is secure and reliable, with all authentication requests verified
against the backend system. It ensures that only authorized users can access the platform.

Forget Password Page

For users who forget their password, a dedicated recovery page is available. Users can enter
their registered email to receive a reset link via email.

Workflow:

User enters their email.
A secure, time-limited link is sent.
The user is redirected to set a new password.
Outcome: The password reset feature improves user convenience while maintaining system
security. It reduces the number of support requests and ensures continued access for users
with forgotten credentials.

The Admin role is one of the most powerful roles in the school system application. It has

Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard is the main control panel for system administrators. It offers a
comprehensive overview of the platform’s activities and system health. The dashboard is
designed with charts, statistics, and quick-action links to important areas of the system.

Features include:

 Total number of users by role (students, teachers, parents, managers, admins)
 Recent system activity and logs
 Graphs now display statistical numbers and percentage distributions of users in Learnova
Outcome: The dashboard helps admins monitor and manage the system in real time, enabling
quick decision-making and access to system-wide insights.

The Members Page is where the admin can view, manage, and control all users registered in

Students

Students are the primary users of the Learnova. Admins can:

Filter by Name or Gender

Add, edit, or remove student accounts

Assign students to Grade

Outcome: The student management feature ensures that all student data is up to date and organized
for academic tracking.

Parents can monitor their children's academic progress. Admins can:

 Manage parent contact information
 Control parent access rights and communications

Other admins may be created to help manage the system. Super admins can:

 Add or remove admin accounts
 Control admin permissions
 Audit system changes made by other admins

Managers

Managers may include heads of departments or school-level coordinators. Admins can:

Schedule Management

Description:
Schedule management allows the admin to create, update, delete and view the weekly class

linking teachers, and ensuring no scheduling conflicts occur.

Key Features:

 Define time slots per day (e.g., 8:00–9:00 AM)
 Assign subjects and teachers to each slot
 Separate schedules for each class or grade
 Conflict-checking to prevent double-booking of teachers or rooms
 Visual calendar or table view for easy monitoring
Result:
This module improves classes organization and ensures students and teachers follow a
structured timetable with no overlaps.

Terms Management

Description:
The admin can define and control the academic terms or semesters (e.g., Term 1 and Term

Key Features:

 Set start and end dates for each term

Key Features:

 Create and name academic years (e.g., 2024–2025)

 Close academic years to freeze data (e.g., grades, attendance)

Result:
Academic year control ensures that the system is up to date and prevents data overlap. It also
allows historical reporting and safe transitions between years.

The admin can create and manage all academic courses and subjects offered in the school.

Key Features:

 Add/Edit/Delete Courses (e.g., Math, Science, History)
 Create/Edit/Delete Subjects under each course
 Assign Courses and Subjects to specific grades and semesters
 Link Teachers to each subject or course
 View Subjects in a structured format by grade or academic year
 Enable/Disable Courses based on the academic year
Result:
The system offers full flexibility in managing the school’s academic structure. Admins can
tailor courses and subjects to match grade levels and term schedules, ensuring proper delivery
and oversight of the curriculum.
